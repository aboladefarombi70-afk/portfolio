About

Numbers into decisions. Ideas into products.

I'm Abolade Oluwadamola Farombi, a data analyst with a BSc in Mathematical Sciences, based in the UK. My work starts where the data is messiest — cleaning raw datasets, writing the SQL to interrogate them, and building Tableau dashboards that turn thousands of rows into insight a business can actually act on.

I'm also the founder of The Alpha DAO, a learning platform I designed and shipped from the ground up. Beyond analysis, I build real, working products through vibe coding — going from an idea to a deployed website without waiting on anyone else to make it happen.

And when it's time to present, I do that too. Public speaking is how I connect the dots for people — explaining not just what the data says, but what it means and what to do next.
